# Holidays in the Maldives

The Maldives is one of the most beautiful destinations in the Indian Ocean for beach holidays, romantic escapes, family vacations and a complete reset by the water. Turquoise lagoons, white-sand beaches, coral reefs, private villas and high-level service create the atmosphere that makes travellers return again and again.

The Republic of Maldives lies in the equatorial waters of the Indian Ocean and consists of more than a thousand islands grouped into atolls. One of the key features of the destination is the “one island — one resort” concept. Many resorts occupy their own private islands, offering privacy, tranquillity and a true sense of escape.

## Why choose a trip to the Maldives?

Trips to the Maldives are mainly chosen for the ocean, natural beauty and a special feeling of seclusion. It is an ideal destination for a honeymoon, a symbolic wedding ceremony, a romantic getaway, a wellness programme or a peaceful family holiday at a high-level resort.

Choosing the right resort in the Maldives is essential. Some islands are better suited for couples and honeymoon travel, while others are ideal for families with children, divers, surfers or guests looking for maximum privacy. It is important to consider not only the hotel category, but also the size of the island, the beach quality, the house reef, meal plan, transfer by seaplane or speedboat, kids club, spa and dining options.

## Weddings and romantic holidays in the Maldives

The Maldives is one of the world’s most popular destinations for honeymoon travel. Here, guests can stay in an overwater villa, arrange a private dinner on the beach, enjoy a symbolic ceremony by the ocean, plan a sunset photo shoot or spend several days in complete privacy.

This format is especially suitable for couples who want more than just a beach holiday — they want a beautiful, carefully planned and deeply personal travel experience.

## Diving, snorkelling and active experiences

The Maldives is known for its underwater world. Coral reefs, tropical fish, turtles, manta rays and other marine life make the destination attractive for diving and snorkelling. Many resorts have professional diving centres offering programmes for both beginners and experienced divers.

Beyond the underwater world, the islands offer water sports, sunset cruises, fishing, boat trips, yoga, spa programmes and memorable dining experiences.

## Climate and seasonality

The Maldives is suitable for holidays throughout the year, as both air and water temperatures remain consistently warm. The most popular season runs from November to April, when there are usually more sunny days, calmer seas and better conditions for beach relaxation.

From May to October, short tropical showers and higher humidity are possible, but this period also has its advantages: attractive resort offers, fewer guests and good conditions for certain water sports.

A holiday in the Maldives is not only about beautiful beaches. The right island, resort, villa category, transfer and service concept are what define how comfortable, private and unforgettable the journey will be.
