## UAE holidays: choosing the right emirate

The United Arab Emirates offers far more than a Dubai city break. Its seven emirates combine beach resorts, desert landscapes, cultural attractions, mountains, dining and family entertainment. The right destination depends on whether the priority is an active city stay, a quiet beach holiday or a multi-stop private itinerary.

### Dubai, Abu Dhabi or Ras Al Khaimah?

Dubai is best suited to travellers looking for beaches, restaurants, shopping, beach clubs and a lively city atmosphere. Palm Jumeirah and the Jumeirah coastline offer resort-style stays, while Downtown Dubai and Dubai Marina work well for guests who want easy access to dining, landmarks and entertainment. A Dubai itinerary can also include a desert resort or private desert experience.

Abu Dhabi offers a quieter and more refined pace. Saadiyat Island combines white-sand beaches with major cultural institutions, while Yas Island is centred around theme parks, sporting venues and family attractions. The emirate works particularly well for travellers who want to combine a luxury beach resort with art, architecture and desert scenery.

Ras Al Khaimah is a strong option for relaxed resort stays, wellness retreats and access to the Hajar Mountains. Fujairah offers a different coastal landscape on the Gulf of Oman, while Ajman and Umm Al Quwain suit travellers looking for a slower pace. Sharjah is particularly appealing for museums, heritage and a more traditional atmosphere.

### Selecting a beach resort

A five-star address is not always a full beachfront resort. Before booking, it is important to confirm whether the property has a private beach, how far it is from the main city districts and which room categories, meal plans and transfer arrangements are included.

Families should consider connecting rooms, shallow-water beaches, children’s clubs and age-appropriate activities. Couples may prefer private villas, club-level accommodation, adults-oriented facilities or a short desert stay away from the city.

### When to visit the UAE

The most comfortable months for beaches, outdoor dining, sightseeing and desert activities generally fall between November and April. Summer travel remains possible, although itineraries are better focused on resort facilities, spas, indoor attractions and air-conditioned cultural venues.

### Tailor-made UAE itineraries

A well-balanced trip can combine Dubai with Abu Dhabi, a city stay with Ras Al Khaimah, or a beach resort with several nights in the desert. Transfer times, room configuration, dining plans, private transport and the number of scheduled activities should all be considered in advance. Leaving enough unplanned time is essential, especially for families and longer resort stays.
