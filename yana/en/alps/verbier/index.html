Holidays in Verbier

Verbier is one of the Swiss Alps’ best-known ski resorts and part of the extensive 4 Vallées area. Its varied terrain, high-mountain views and lively après-ski atmosphere appeal to confident skiers, freeride enthusiasts and groups seeking both mountains and dining.

Skiing, Mont-Fort and winter activities

From Verbier, the Four Valleys slopes lead towards Bruson, La Tzoumaz, Nendaz, Veysonnaz and Thyon. Mont-Fort is renowned for its mountain panorama. Off-piste outings call for a qualified guide and attention to current conditions.

Hotels, chalets and travel style

Travellers can stay in a hotel close to the centre and lifts or choose a serviced chalet in a quieter setting. Lift access, dining, group size and desired privacy all affect the choice.

Lessons, winter walks and spa time can round out the journey. Transfers and ski passes are best organised in advance, leaving space to relax after a full day in the mountains.
