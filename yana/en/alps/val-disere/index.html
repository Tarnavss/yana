Tailor-made ski holidays in Val d’Isère

Val d’Isère is a high-altitude French Alpine resort where energetic skiing meets the character of an old mountain village, high-end hotels and chalets. It suits travellers who put varied terrain and a strong ski programme at the centre of their holiday.

Skiing in Val d’Isère and Tignes

The linked Tignes–Val d’Isère area offers more than 300 km of pistes. Routes span Solaise, Bellevarde, Le Fornet, Pissaillas and La Daille. The Face de Bellevarde is a celebrated sporting descent, while less experienced guests can choose suitable runs and lessons.

Where to stay in Val d’Isère

The choice of hotel, residence or chalet depends on access to lifts and the village centre, group size and preferred service. A property by the slopes works well for an active week; a private chalet can offer more space for family or friends.

After skiing

Restaurants, spas and walks through the old village add to a day in the mountains. Transfers, ski passes, ski school and restaurant reservations are worth arranging before departure, especially for peak winter dates.
