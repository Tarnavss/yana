Holidays in Megève

Megève is an Alpine resort in France’s Haute-Savoie, where skiing is paired with the atmosphere of a traditional mountain village, fine dining and views of Mont Blanc. It belongs to the extensive Évasion Mont-Blanc ski area, whose varied slopes can suit experienced skiers and families alike.

Megève has an intimate village character. After skiing, the day continues among the restaurants, boutiques and inviting streets of its centre, while the hotels and chalets retain the aesthetic of the French Alps.

Hotels, chalets and skiing in Megève

The resort offers landmark hotels, spa retreats and Alpine chalets. Accommodation options include Four Seasons Hotel Megève, Les Fermes de Marie, Flocons de Sel and Les Chalets du Mont d’Arbois.

A trip can be tailored around your choice of hotel or chalet, transfers, ski passes, lessons and restaurant reservations. Megève is particularly appealing to travellers who want to combine Alpine skiing with gastronomy, spa time and the relaxed pace of a French mountain village.
