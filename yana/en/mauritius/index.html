Luxury Tours to Mauritius: A Tailor-Made Indian Ocean Journey

Mauritius is an Indian Ocean island where beach holidays can be combined with nature experiences, golf, water sports, gastronomy and the country’s diverse cultural heritage. It is suitable for a relaxing family holiday, a romantic escape, a destination wedding or an itinerary combining the beach with excursions and outdoor activities.

Tailor-made holidays in Mauritius are organised based on the number and age of the guests, travel dates, preferred level of privacy, length of stay and budget. The focus is not only on selecting a hotel, but also on choosing the most suitable coast, beach, room or villa category, meal plan, transfers and additional services.

Who Is Mauritius Best Suited For?

Mauritius is ideal for travellers who want to combine a high-quality beach holiday with a varied programme beyond the hotel.

For families with children, resorts with easy access to the water, sheltered lagoons, spacious family accommodation, children’s clubs and suitable facilities are recommended. For couples, intimate hotels, private villas, romantic dinners, cruises and private excursions are popular choices.

Mauritius is also well suited to honeymoons, destination weddings, golf holidays, diving, snorkelling, kitesurfing, yacht trips and journeys through the island’s natural landscapes.

When Is the Best Time to Visit Mauritius?

Mauritius is considered a year-round destination, although weather conditions and the style of holiday vary depending on the season and the chosen coast.

From May to October, the weather is generally cooler and drier. This period is well suited to excursions, hiking, golf and outdoor activities. From November to April, temperatures are higher, humidity increases and the ocean is usually warmer.

When choosing travel dates, it is important to consider more than average temperatures. The hotel’s location, prevailing winds, lagoon conditions and planned activities can all affect the experience. For this reason, the best season should be determined according to the specific itinerary and holiday format.

Which Coast of Mauritius Should You Choose?

The different regions of Mauritius offer distinct atmospheres, beaches and holiday experiences.

Northern Mauritius is suitable for travellers who want to combine the beach with restaurants, shopping, nightlife and boat trips. Grand Baie is known for its lively resort atmosphere and wide choice of restaurants and entertainment.

The east coast features long sandy beaches, spacious lagoons, secluded resorts and golf courses. It is often selected for peaceful family holidays and romantic escapes.

Western Mauritius is known for its sunsets, nature experiences, diving, snorkelling and kitesurfing. It also provides convenient access to Le Morne, Chamarel Waterfall and Black River Gorges National Park.

The south of the island offers a more natural and secluded atmosphere. This area includes quiet beaches, fishing villages, nature reserves, tea plantations and Blue Bay Marine Park.

Hotels and Private Villas in Mauritius

Mauritius offers a wide range of accommodation, including large resorts with extensive facilities, golf resorts, family-friendly hotels, private villas and smaller properties designed for a more secluded stay.

When selecting accommodation, attention is paid not only to the official hotel category, but also to the quality of the beach, lagoon conditions, room location, privacy, restaurants, facilities for children, spa services, golf courses and access to the island’s main natural attractions.

This approach helps avoid situations in which a formally high-end hotel does not match the guests’ actual expectations.
