## Holidays in St. Moritz

St. Moritz is a legendary Alpine resort in Switzerland’s Engadin valley, where winter sports meet landmark hotels, gastronomy and the distinctive setting of a mountain lake. One of the birthplaces of winter tourism in the Alps, it remains among Switzerland’s best-known resorts.

The surrounding ski region offers terrain for different abilities. Its principal areas — **Corviglia, Corvatsch and Diavolezza** — allow for both experienced skiers and family winter holidays.

### Hotels and winter holidays in St. Moritz

St. Moritz is known for historic grand hotels and contemporary mountain retreats. Landmark addresses include **Badrutt’s Palace Hotel, Kulm Hotel St. Moritz, Carlton Hotel St. Moritz and Suvretta House**.

A stay can combine skiing with spa time, dining, lakeside walks and resort events. The choice of accommodation should take account of lift access, the group’s preferences and the transfers between ski areas.
