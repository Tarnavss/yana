## Holidays in Alta Badia

Alta Badia is a ski region in the heart of the Dolomites, where time on the slopes meets Ladin culture, Alpine hospitality and exceptional gastronomy. It forms part of **Dolomiti Superski** and provides access to the **Sellaronda** route around the Sella massif.

The villages of **Corvara, Colfosco, La Villa and San Cassiano** differ in atmosphere and proximity to the lifts. Panoramic runs can be combined with gentler family routes, while more experienced skiers may be drawn to the **Gran Risa** piste.

### Gastronomy, hotels and travel style

Mountain restaurants and local cuisine are central to an Alta Badia holiday. The choice between a family hotel, intimate spa retreat and private chalet depends on the group, lift access and desired privacy.

Beyond skiing, the programme can include winter walks, spa time and visits to neighbouring valleys. Accommodation, transfers, ski passes and lessons are best planned together, especially for an itinerary that covers more than one Dolomite resort.
