## Holidays in Courchevel

Courchevel is one of the best-known ski resorts in the French Alps and forms part of the extensive **Les 3 Vallées** ski area. The resort combines slopes for different levels of experience with iconic hotels, Alpine chalets, fine dining and a high level of personalised service.

The most celebrated area, **Courchevel 1850**, is known for convenient access to the slopes, exceptional hotels, restaurants and a sophisticated resort atmosphere. Travellers looking for a quieter setting can choose one of Courchevel’s other villages while remaining connected to the wider Three Valleys ski area.

### Hotels, chalets and skiing in Courchevel

Accommodation ranges from landmark hotels close to the pistes to spacious Alpine chalets suitable for families and groups of friends. Each journey can be tailored to individual skiing experience and preferences, with transfers, ski passes, private lessons, restaurant reservations and additional resort services arranged in advance.

Courchevel works equally well for a dedicated ski holiday or a winter escape combining time on the slopes with spa experiences, gastronomy, shopping and the distinctive atmosphere of the French Alps.
