## Holidays in Zermatt

Zermatt lies beneath the Matterhorn, combining high-altitude skiing with landmark hotels, chalets and the atmosphere of a traditional Swiss mountain village. Conventional private cars do not circulate in the resort centre, so transfers and luggage delivery are worth planning in advance.

The ski area includes **Sunnegga–Rothorn, Gornergrat and Matterhorn Glacier Paradise**. Subject to conditions, an itinerary can extend across the border to **Breuil-Cervinia and Valtournenche** in Italy.

### Hotels, chalets and skiing in Zermatt

The right accommodation depends on lift access, Matterhorn views and the desired level of privacy. Landmark options include **Grand Hotel Zermatterhof, Riffelalp Resort 2222m, Mont Cervin Palace and Chalet Zermatt Peak**.

A tailored trip can include transfers, ski passes, lessons, dining, spa time and journeys on the mountain railways. Zermatt suits both intensive skiing and a slower Alpine holiday focused on scenery and gastronomy.
