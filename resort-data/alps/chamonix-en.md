## Ski holidays in Chamonix

Chamonix-Mont-Blanc is an Alpine resort beneath Mont Blanc, known for high-mountain scenery, a sporting character and a long mountaineering heritage. Its lively centre offers restaurants, shops and a wider range of accommodation than a small mountain village.

### The ski areas of Chamonix

Skiing is spread across **Brévent, Flégère, Grands Montets, Balme and Les Houches**. Each area has a different setting and terrain, so accommodation and daily transfers should be matched to skiing ability and the trip’s priorities.

### Aiguille du Midi and the Vallée Blanche

The ascent to **Aiguille du Midi** reveals dramatic high-mountain views. The **Vallée Blanche** is a glacial itinerary away from prepared pistes; it calls for a mountain guide and suitable conditions.

### Accommodation and experiences beyond skiing

Stay in central Chamonix, choose a quieter base in Les Praz or Argentière, or book a chalet for family and friends. Mountain walks, dining and spa time can round out the journey. Transfers between ski areas, lessons and excursions are best arranged in advance.
