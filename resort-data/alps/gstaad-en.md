## Holidays in Gstaad

Gstaad is an Alpine resort in Switzerland’s Saanenland, known for traditional architecture, chalets, historic hotels and a relaxed atmosphere. It appeals to travellers who value attentive service, village walks and a winter holiday at their own pace.

The region’s ski areas offer routes for different abilities. A stay can centre on active skiing or combine the slopes with gastronomy, spa time and walks through the Swiss Alpine scenery.

### Hotels, chalets and winter holidays in Gstaad

Well-known hotels include **Gstaad Palace, The Alpina Gstaad, Le Grand Bellevue and Park Gstaad**. A private chalet may suit a family or a group of friends, with services arranged in advance.

When planning the trip, consider the hotel’s position relative to the lifts and village centre, transfers, ski passes, lessons and dining. These details help shape either an active ski week or a more leisurely mountain escape.
