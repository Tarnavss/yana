## Holidays in Cortina d’Ampezzo

Cortina d’Ampezzo is one of Italy’s best-known mountain resorts, set among the landscapes of the Dolomites. Skiing here goes hand in hand with Italian gastronomy, elegant hotels, walks through town and an engaging resort atmosphere.

The surrounding slopes vary in character, allowing for a gentle family day or more active skiing. Dolomite panoramas are a defining part of the experience, and a winter itinerary can include mountain walks and dining stops.

### Hotels and skiing in Cortina d’Ampezzo

When choosing a hotel, consider its distance from the lifts and the centre, transfer arrangements and your preferred pace. Spacious rooms, residences or a private chalet may work well for families and groups of friends.

A tailored programme can bring together accommodation, transfers, ski passes, lessons, restaurants and time away from the slopes. This makes room for both the sporting appeal of Cortina and its Italian style.
